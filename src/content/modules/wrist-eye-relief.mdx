## Wrist Circles & Stretches
**Duration:** 45 seconds

Extend arms forward. Make circles with your wrists—10 in each direction. Then gently pull fingers back with opposite hand. Hold each stretch for 3 breaths. These quick yoga stretches prevent repetitive strain from typing.

## Eye Palming & Movements
**Duration:** 75 seconds

Rub palms together to create warmth. Cup them over closed eyes without pressure. Rest here for 30 seconds. Then, eyes open, look slowly up, down, left, right. Finish with wide circles. This 2 minute yoga sequence combats screen fatigue and computer eye strain.

## Voice-Over Script

Let them rest in this healing space. Gently move your gaze—up, down, side to side. You're nourishing your body's most overworked tools.
