## Standing Forward Fold
**Duration:** 45 seconds

Stand with feet hip-width apart. Hinge at your hips and fold forward, letting your head and arms hang heavy. Bend your knees generously. Sway gently side to side. This quick yoga workout reverses the effects of prolonged sitting.

## Calf Raises & Ankle Circles
**Duration:** 60 seconds

Come to standing. Rise up onto your toes, then lower down. Repeat 10 times. Then lift one foot and circle your ankle 5 times each direction. Switch feet. These quick yoga stretches boost circulation instantly.

## Standing Side Stretch & Hip Circles
**Duration:** 75 seconds

Interlace fingers overhead. Stretch to the right, then left. Hold each for 3 breaths. Place hands on hips and make large circles with your hips—5 in each direction. This fast yoga routine is perfect for afternoon slumps.

## Voice-Over Script

Stand up and shake off stagnation. Fold forward and let gravity do the work—feel your spine lengthen. Press into your feet as you rise onto your toes—you're activating circulation. Now stretch side to side, creating space in your ribcage. Circle your hips and wake up your lower body. Energy is flowing again.
