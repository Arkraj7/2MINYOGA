## Seated Spinal Twist (Right)
**Duration:** 90 seconds

Sit tall at the edge of your chair. Inhale to lengthen your spine. Exhale and twist to the right, bringing left hand to right knee. Right hand rests behind you. Hold and breathe deeply. This office chair yoga twist releases spinal tension.

## Seated Spinal Twist (Left)
**Duration:** 90 seconds

Return to center. Inhale tall. Exhale and twist to the left, bringing right hand to left knee. Left hand rests behind you. Breathe space into your spine.

## Gentle Side Bend
**Duration:** 60 seconds (30s each)

Inhale arms overhead. Exhale, reach right arm up and over to the left. Hold for 3 breaths. Repeat on the other side. This quick tension relief yoga stretch opens your entire side body.

## Voice-Over Script

Let's restore clarity and space in your body. Sit tall and grow even taller on your inhale. As you twist, imagine wringing out tension from your spine. Breathe into your back body. Each exhale deepens the twist just slightly. Return to center and twist to the other side. Now stretch side to side, creating length through your entire torso.
