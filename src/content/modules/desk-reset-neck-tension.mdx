## Seated Cat-Cow
**Duration:** 30 seconds

Sit tall in your chair. Inhale, arch your back and lift your chest. Exhale, round your spine. Flow gently with your breath. This quick yoga stretch mobilizes your entire spine in under a minute.

## Gentle Neck Rolls
**Duration:** 45 seconds

Drop your right ear toward right shoulder. Hold for 3 breaths. Slowly roll chin toward chest, then left ear to left shoulder. Repeat 2-3 times. These office desk yoga rolls release computer neck tension instantly.

## Eagle Arms Shoulder Release
**Duration:** 60 seconds (30s each side)

Wrap right arm under left, bring palms together. Lift elbows to shoulder height. Hold and breathe deeply. Switch sides. This fast yoga routine targets the upper back where desk workers hold the most stress.
