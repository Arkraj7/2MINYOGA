## Comfortable Seated Position
**Duration:** 5 minutes

Sit with your spine tall but relaxed. Place one hand on your heart, one on your belly. Breathe in through your nose for 5 counts, out through your nose for 5 counts. Maintain this rhythm for 5 minutes. This meditation yoga stress relief technique optimizes your nervous system reset.

## Voice-Over Script

Settle into a comfortable seat. Place your hands gently—one on your heart, one on your belly. Breathe in for five counts—one, two, three, four, five. Breathe out for five—one, two, three, four, five. This is your natural rhythm. This fast yoga routine for stress management brings your heart and mind into coherence. Each cycle deepens your calm.
