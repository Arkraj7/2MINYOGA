## Nadi Shodhana (Alternate Nostril Breathing)
**Duration:** 3 minutes

Sit comfortably. Use your right thumb to close your right nostril. Inhale through left nostril for 4 counts. Close both nostrils briefly. Release right nostril, exhale for 4 counts. Inhale through right for 4. Close both. Exhale through left for 4. This is one round. Repeat for 3 minutes. This meditation yoga stress relief technique calms acute anxiety.

## Voice-Over Script

Pause briefly. Release and breathe out through the right. Now in through the right. Out through the left. This is your rhythm. Each cycle brings greater balance and calm.
