## Seated Figure-4 Stretch
**Duration:** 2 minutes (1 min each side)

Sit near the edge of your chair. Cross your right ankle over your left knee, creating a figure-4 shape. Flex your right foot. Gently hinge forward from your hips. Hold for 1 minute. Switch sides. This quick yoga stretch targets deep hip tension from sitting.

## Seated Hip Circles
**Duration:** 90 seconds

Sit tall. Imagine a pen attached to your tailbone. Draw large circles on the chair with that pen. Make 5 circles in each direction, moving slowly and mindfully.

## Seated Cat-Cow with Side Bend
**Duration:** 90 seconds

Flow through cat-cow 5 times. Then, place right hand on chair beside you. Inhale left arm up and over to the right. Hold for 3 breaths. Repeat on the other side. This office desk yoga sequence releases lower back compression.

## Voice-Over Script

Let's release the lower back and hips—where sitting leaves its mark. Create your figure-4 shape and fold forward gently. Breathe into your outer hip. Move to the other side with care. Now, move your hips in smooth circles, mobilizing your sacrum and lumbar spine. Finally, flow through cat-cow and stretch side to side. Your lower body is opening, releasing, renewing.
